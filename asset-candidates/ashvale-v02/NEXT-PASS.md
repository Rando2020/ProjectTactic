# Asset production brief for the next pass

Continue from the editable master and build script. Produce original painterly dark-fantasy assets with bold silhouettes, deliberate planes, restrained blue steel, worn brass, oxblood cloth and warm focal lighting. Arcane and No Rest for the Wicked are visual influences; do not extract their assets or reproduce their characters.

## Character priority

The current helmeted character is a rig and silhouette candidate, not a final Zane likeness. Replace block hands with gripping anatomy, refine helmet/face design against the game's character reference, reduce rectangular armor shapes, and paint material-specific wear instead of applying one steel image uniformly. Preserve the established 1.93 m height unless the production character specification says otherwise.

Keep the weapon grip fixed through the cycle. Develop anticipation, strike and recovery, inspect the blade path at several frames, and obtain the game's actual damage-event timing before authoring animation events. Improve planted foot contacts; do not report the walk as final until inspected in motion.

## Environment priority

Use the existing 2 m grid and 1 m height step. Improve the courtyard with fewer repeated brush patterns, intentional edge wear, broken silhouettes and a readable focal arch. Keep floor readability clear beneath the character. Test art at the real tactical camera before adding small detail.

## Delivery gate

Export candidate GLBs, retain editable source and texture provenance, reimport into a clean Blender scene, run mesh QA, import into the isolated Godot preview, and test clips/movement. Inspect actual rendered meshes. Record every unresolved limitation. Coordinate production skeleton, renderer, collision and animation conventions with NOVA through the user-provided integration channel; do not assume this candidate matches production.
