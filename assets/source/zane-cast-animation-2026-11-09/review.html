Lossless transparent loop

The APNG retains the original 1254 × 1254 RGBA source pixels. No warping, optical-flow frames or crossfades are used. These two views are presentation scaling only.

  • Lift 80 ms → windup 120 ms → extend 80 ms → release 240 ms → extend 80 ms → windup 80 ms → lift 80 ms → idle.
  • Presentation release event at 280 ms. Casting locks facing; repeated starts are ignored; cancel returns to idle.
  • 60 Godot headless checks pass, including the importable SpriteFrames resource.
  • All 100 APNG frames exactly match their corresponding source RGBA pixels. MP4 is 300 frames, six seconds, 1280 × 720.
  • Source frames and sampled encoded video phases were visually inspected. Browser playback and graphical Godot playback have not been inspected in this environment.
