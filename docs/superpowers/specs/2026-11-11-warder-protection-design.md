# Warder protection runtime

## Intent and authority
Make the existing Cover and Iron Wall catalog skills work in both Godot runtimes. The user requested continued implementation of the existing classes, passives and skills. This slice follows AbilityDB and JobTreeData on main 67be5c6, not the incompatible legacy React/Temper-cost design. No new classes, paid dependencies, save migration, skill ownership or unlock-order changes.

## Chosen contract and explicit implementation decisions
- Cover costs 0 MP and the ordinary action. It selects another living ally at Manhattan range 1, extended to 2 by earned Warder mastery. This resolves raw range 2 versus the catalog's adjacent description and mastery extension in favor of the description/mastery pair.
- One protector covers one ally; an already covered ally or already covering caster cannot acquire another link until the old link expires. A miss does not consume Cover. A weak, battle-local link expires at the protector's next begin_turn, not the ally's turn. Repeated/freed/dead references cannot leave active protection.
- Direct hostile physical or magical attacks, including ranged attacks and direct counters/Echo Strike, intercept once. Area/chain/Cleave/Piercing Line, terrain, DoT and true damage do not redirect. There is no recursive interception and no ally relocation. Range and a living, unstunned/unpetrified protector are checked at contact; moving apart suspends interception until range is restored or expiry.
- Attack aim, height and facing context remain the originally selected tile. The interceptor supplies resistance, affinity and armor. This is interception at the aimed tile, not a free movement or retargeting action. Hit/crit RNG count is unchanged. The actual recipient owns HP/armor loss, hit statuses, elite effects, CT delay, counter eligibility, kill effects and contact feedback. Ground effects remain on the originally aimed tile.
- Forecasts identify the protector, show its HP and armor, and name the protected ally. Selection and range still address the original tile. Enemy intent cannot promise a KO on the untouched ally.
- Iron Wall costs 10 MP, is self-only, and multiplies normal physical/magical incoming damage by 0.6 before armor. It stacks multiplicatively after existing mastery, Protect and Defend with the existing rounding convention. True damage, terrain and DoT keep their existing bypass behavior.
- The cast turn is excluded from Iron Wall's two future owner-turn duration by the existing self-buff duration offset. While active, actions and counterattacks are blocked, including direct/enemy/auto execution and state changes during anticipation. Move and Wait remain available. It cannot stack or refresh while active.
- Keep Cover and Iron Wall available through existing acquisition. The courtyard may expose these already-authored skills as battle-local practice options, without granting them in saves. Feedback is honest generic aura/text, not a claim of new painted animation or new artwork.

## Approaches
A resolver-only redirect is too narrow: it leaves status, death, counter and presentation consumers pointing at the wrong target. A global Unit.receive_damage redirect is too broad: it redirects environmental/area damage and changes unrelated callers. Use a one-hop read-only recipient query shared by combat math and resolution, explicit area exclusions, and authoritative recipient references on outcomes for manager consumers. Add focused status/action gates without silently redefining other status behavior.
