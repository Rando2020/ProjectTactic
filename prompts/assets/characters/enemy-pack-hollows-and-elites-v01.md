## Prompt

Create an original enemy family called Hollows: people-shaped remnants whose solved emotions left deliberate absences in armor, cloth, and posture. Three-quarter isometric tactical view, transparent 128 × 128 canvas, feet bottom-center, southwest light, readable at browser scale. Their horror comes from missing identity rather than gore: empty face planes, repeated civic insignia rubbed smooth, restrained violet-black seams, worn iron and ash cloth.

Produce separate idle and attack frames for Hollow Lancer, Hollow Cantor, Hollow Bulwark, and Hollow Stray. Then create elite variants for Lancer and Cantor using increased silhouette mass, a broken pale-gold halo fragment, and one additional readable armor feature. Elites must read through shape before glow or color.
