# Battle VFX Core Pack v01

- Asset ID: `vfx-battle-core`
- Intended use: compact browser-safe combat feedback
- Generation tool: image generator or hand-authored sprite-sheet workflow
- Generation date: fill at generation time
- Status: prompt-ready

## Prompt

Create a coherent core VFX set for an isometric dark-fantasy tactical RPG: weapon impact, fire impact, ice impact, lightning impact, earth impact, wind impact, memory-gold heal, violet curse, shield break, and unit defeat dissolve. Transparent background, centered origin, high contrast at small size, painterly pixel-adjacent edges, brief anticipation, dominant impact, fast clean decay. Effects use restrained sparks and manuscript-like strokes rather than photoreal smoke. Preserve unit silhouette through the effect.

Export one horizontal 6-frame sprite sheet per effect, 128 × 128 per frame, frames ordered left to right, consistent center and baseline. Name each `vfx-{effect}-core-v01.png`.

## Exclusions

No camera background, floor plane, UI frame, text, lens flare, full-screen bloom, realistic gore, excessive smoke, or particles touching adjacent frames.

## Review checklist

- Exactly six equally sized frames
- First and last frames have low visual mass
- Impact reads at 50 percent scale
- Alpha edges do not fringe on dark backgrounds
